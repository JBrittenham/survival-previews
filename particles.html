FIXED CAMERAS / MATCHED CLOCK / DAY AND DUSK

A little movement in the air.

Compare the same clearing with particles off and on. The bounded meadow effect adds small drifting motes and flecks; vegetation, shared wind, camera, clock and lighting are matched within each pair. This is an optional visual study, not yet an accepted default.

Press play on each clip. Each contains eight seconds of deterministic motion at 30 fps; this is the playback rate, not measured game FPS. Videos do not autoplay. Use fullscreen or the MP4 links for a closer view; the first-frame PNGs are native 1920 × 1080 captures.

Daylight clearing

Dusk clearing

Scope and limitations. Only the atmospheric particle layer changes. It adds no collision, lights, shadows, gameplay or networking state. This meadow study does not establish indoor occlusion or support for other biomes. Grass shape and distance limitations remain separate work. These fixed-camera clips isolate motion and are not a walking/gameplay test. Readback, PNG encoding and video playback are not rendering-performance measurements; performance evidence is recorded separately. Earlier material comparisons and lighting controls remain available. This website contains previews, not a playable mobile game.
