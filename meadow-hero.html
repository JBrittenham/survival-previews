MATCHED VIEWS / DAY AND DUSK

One tree, grass rhythm and light.

This isolated, opt-in study compares a baseline with a provisional candidate. It tests canopy shape, grass curvature and lighting together; it does not establish which individual change is responsible for an improvement. Judge stylized shape, readable light and shade, ground transitions and low-poly character.

Both study versions add one non-harvestable scenery tree and omit authored production groves; ordinary resource trees remain. Normal gameplay defaults and saved world data remain unchanged. Particles are off. Cameras, loaded-world extent, clock, HDR and anti-aliasing match within each pair. These captures use the study fresh-world default seed; the reports do not independently record it. Geometry counts may differ. The candidate has not been declared final or artistically accepted.

Tap an image for the native 1920 × 1080 PNG. The smaller JPEG previews help phone loading but are not pixel-exact evidence.
