Scope and limitations. Only grass and nearby tree-foliage deformation change. Combined distant-forest canopies, cloud shadows, water and physics remain unchanged; no particles are added. Grass silhouette repetition, short render distance and normals that do not bend with vertices remain known limitations. These fixed-camera clips isolate motion and are not a walking/gameplay test. Readback, PNG encoding and video playback are not rendering-performance measurements; performance evidence is recorded separately. Earlier material comparisons and lighting controls remain available. This website contains previews, not a playable mobile game.
